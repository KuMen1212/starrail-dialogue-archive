### Story Recap

When the Lord Ravager Asat Pramad unveiled his disguise, his clones appeared across Planarcadia. Asat Pramad made a spectacular entry into the Phantasmoon Games, sparking widespread panic and turning Planarcadia into an apocalyptic ruin.

Meanwhile, Asat Pramad led Himeko into a painting that represented her past. Asat Pramad did this to seek out an answer: At the end of the Bloodstained Games fifteen years ago, how did Himeko escape the Weathering Curse, and what was Aha's role in it all?

Himeko's past was slowly revealed: She and Motoko, the imagenae who bore her wishes, struggled to ignite the Express and send it soaring into the sky. Though Aha offered no reward, they received a "gift" from a mysterious figure hidden within the shadow of the Phantasmoon: Himeko and Motoko exchanged their destinies.

Two girls made a promise to each other: When the Phantasmoon was full again, the Express would return with companions and stories. With a desperate determination to get even closer to the stars, the Astral Express soared into the sky once more. Fifteen years later, Himeko had become the person she had once aspired to be.

In this game between Asat Pramad and Himeko, Himeko left behind clues only the Express Crew would comprehend, and revealed the true nature of Asat Pramad's abilities: As a Lord Ravager with power over possibilities, he had imagenated an apocalyptic ending dooming everyone on the "canvas" of reality in Ahatopia.

Yao Guang summoned the Spiritus Kālacakra Skyplume to battle Asat Pramad's countless created selves. By using the power of "observation," she located Asat Pramad's true body and sunk into the gap between existence and non-existence. And so, the battle between the Express Crew and Asat Pramad began.

In the ensuing battle, the Express Crew found themselves pushed to their limits against a Lord Ravager fighting at full strength. Fulfilling her promise as the navigator, Himeko stood before everyone, willing to sacrifice her life to protect their chance of escape.

When all hope seemed lost, my desperate wish awakened the Stellaron. I chose to bring Himeko back, and with her, preserve the continuation of the Trailblaze.

The Stellaron answered this phenomenal wish, sending me back to the moment before everything became irreversible and allowing me to turn the tide of the battle. With the encouragement of the Express Crew, Himeko channeled unprecedented Wishpower and confidence to imagenate something she had long been unable to materialize: the mechatron "Starblazer."

Asat Pramad's plan was foiled. Even in his moment of defeat, he arrogantly declared to me: Flee. His plan stretched beyond victory and defeat. He had plotted for so long, and his plan was finally coming to fruition. The Voracity husk sealed for ages by the Phantasmoon Games was finally awakening.
