### Story Recap

After Nihilux revealed her identity as an Aeon to us, we had a deep conversation. In the end, she approved of my plan to restart the Phantasmoon Games.

After accompanying Nihilux deep into the tavern to retrieve Aha's mask, we once again went to the Life Sciences Institute, where Yabuli, head of the Technology Department, was located, in search of bait that could lure the Voracity: Leviathan Essence.

Arriving alongside us were Ashveil and Kuchiba, sent by Pearl. But their goal was entirely different from ours, they were here to assassinate Yabuli.

Various records in the Life Sciences Institute proved that Yabuli had been studying leviathans, and single-handedly brought about the blood debt of Planarcadia. Now, she took an even greater risk by connecting herself to the Lord Ravager Asat Pramad's die in hopes of obtaining the wisdom of leviathans.

Such an extreme act could only bring endless calamity. Lighthouse, having the truth revealed to him by Asat Pramad's Die, injected an excessive amount of Leviathan Essence into Yabuli, pushing her beyond reason and transforming her into a raving beast.

At the moment of crisis, Kuchiba, the last descendant of the leviathans, stopped the crazed Yabuli. She left us with countless unanswered questions before quietly drifting away. And Lighthouse, having learned the truth of the past, sacrificed everything and chose a hero's ending.

In the end, Pearl's plan was realized in full. She came to understand the true essence of artistic creation and fulfilled her revenge against the murderer. With every condition finally in place, she used the power of her art to open a passage between Astropolis and Planarcadia, where she quietly awaited our return.

The Supplicants gathered once again beneath the sacred tree of the Courtyard, and the Elation summoned her gondola, inviting everyone to embark on a great journey to hunt the Voracity.
