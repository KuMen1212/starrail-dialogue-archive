### Story Recap

After vanquishing the Lord Ravager Asat Pramad, we could not prevent his plan from coming to pass. As the world shook, the cast-off husk of Voracity, sealed deep within Ahatopia's core for ages untold, began to stir once more.

Pearl entrusted the Express Crew with the responsibility of ensuring Planarcadia's survival. I was tasked with heading to the Interastral Peace Entertainment's headquarters and the site of the upcoming Anti-Destruction Alliance summit — Astropolis.

Pearl wished to present the crisis at a summit of the IPC's seven department heads, and use their decision to draw the Amber Lord's attention. After which, the power of the Preservation could be used to seal the awakening husk.

On the contrary, the Marketing Development Department worked with the Technology Department to propose an even riskier plan: They would attempt to turn the husk into a weapon and use it to resist the forces of the Destruction.

In the end, Mr. Arita of the Traditional Project Department held the final decisive vote for the two opposing plans. To secure it, we traveled far and wide. Yet while struggling against impossible odds, we also witnessed firsthand both the malice and the crippling complacency lurking within what is the IPC's sprawling bureaucratic machine.

After exposing the Marketing Development Department's tricks, Nihilux and I finally met Mr. Arita. Louis Fleming, the IPC founder, unexpectedly appeared to involve himself in the affair. In the end, Planarcadia was saved!

And all of these astonishing coincidences were actually made possible by Nihilux, the comic artist who had been concealing her true identity all along. She now calls herself the Aeon of Elation, Aha!
